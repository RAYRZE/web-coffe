Our Story

More than just
a coffee shop.

Bersua means "to meet" — and that's exactly what we designed this space for. Opened in October 2023, our two-storey hideaway in Matraman blends indoor warmth, outdoor calm, and a rooftop escape. Whether you're here to work, catch up, or simply be, you'll find a seat that feels like yours.
